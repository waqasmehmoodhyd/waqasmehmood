What Is a Technical SEO Audit?

A technical SEO audit is a review of website infrastructure factors that affect how search engine bots crawl, render, index, and rank web pages. It evaluates indexability status codes, robots.txt directives, XML sitemaps, canonical tags, redirect chains, JSON-LD structured data, and mobile Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) to eliminate architectural bottlenecks.

What is a technical SEO audit and how often should it be conducted?

A technical SEO audit evaluates infrastructure factors affecting crawlability, indexation, rendering, and Core Web Vitals. An in-depth audit should be performed quarterly or before any major website redesign or CMS migration.

How long does it take to see real results from SEO?

Technical SEO fixes and indexation corrections show diagnostic improvements within 2 to 4 weeks. High-intent commercial rankings typically mature within 3 to 6 months as topical authority builds.

What makes your 27 in-house SEO tools different from SaaS tools?

Can you manage both Google Ads and Organic SEO simultaneously?

Yes, combining PPC with organic SEO is optimal. Converting search query data from Google Ads directly informs the organic content strategy, while first-page organic rankings allow reducing expensive ad spend on high-volume terms.

How does Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) differ from traditional SEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) focuses on structuring content with direct factual definitions, semantic JSON-LD schema, and verifiable entity proof so AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can accurately cite your website as an authoritative source.
