Strict Zero-Telemetry Privacy Guarantee

The Detailed SEO & AI Engine extension operates completely client-side in your local browser sandbox. It does NOT collect, store, transmit, monetize, or sell your personal information, browsing history, form inputs, or search queries. No external telemetry servers, analytics pixels, or tracking cookies are ever contacted.

1. Introduction & Single Purpose Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Chrome browser extension "Waqas Mehmood - Detailed SEO & AI AEO Engine 2026" (hereinafter referred to as "the Extension"), created by independent SEO Specialist & Software Engineer Waqas Mehmood.

In strict compliance with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, the Extension operates with a clear, single-purpose functionality: to inspect the on-page HTML structure (title tags, meta descriptions, headings hierarchy, JSON-LD schema, link architecture, and AEO answer engine signals) of the webpage actively viewed by the user upon request.

2. Data Collection Policy (Zero Data Collected)

We uphold a strict zero-data collection standard. When you install and use the Extension, we collect:

  • NO Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical locations, or payment details.
  • NO Browsing History: We do not log, track, or record the URLs you visit. The extension only inspects the active tab's DOM when you explicitly open the extension popup.
  • NO Keystrokes or Form Data: We do not monitor user input, passwords, credit card numbers, or form submissions.
  • NO Cookies or Authentication Tokens: We do not read or access your cookies, session IDs, or login credentials.
  • NO IP Addresses: We do not log your IP address or fingerprint your device.

3. Technical Operation (100% In-Memory Local Processing)

All document object model (DOM) parsing, heading counts, JSON-LD schema inspection, and AEO citability calculations occur 100% locally in your device's random-access memory (RAM) within Chrome's isolated content script sandbox.

When you close the extension popup or navigate away from the tab, all parsed diagnostic data is immediately erased from memory. At no point is webpage content transmitted to external cloud servers, remote databases, or third-party APIs.

4. Permissions Justification (Chrome Web Store Compliance)

The Extension requires three minimal permissions declared in its manifest.json. Each permission is strictly used to fulfill its single core purpose:

Permission Google Definition Exact Usage in This Extension
activeTab Temporary access to active tab Enables the extension to read the HTML title, headings, meta tags, and schema scripts of the page currently open in your active tab when you click the extension icon.
scripting Execute scripts on webpages Injects the local content script (content.js) into the active tab to extract On-Page SEO and AI citability metrics without reloading the page.
storage Store client-side data Stores client-side user preferences (such as your chosen display format or previously viewed audit scores) strictly inside your local browser via chrome.storage.local. Never uploaded to the cloud.
