Future Roadmap: Upcoming Features of Detailed SEO & AI Engine

We’re constantly iterating. Here’s what’s coming in the next 12 months.

Beyond keyword density, the engine will evaluate readability, topical depth, and “perplexity‑adjusted” relevance using the latest Gemini 2.0 models.

Firefox and Edge builds will be distributed via their respective add‑on stores, preserving the same zero‑telemetry guarantees.

A SaaS‑hosted dashboard for teams to aggregate audit results across domains, with role‑based access and CSV + JSON export.

The extension will propose one‑click JavaScript patches for common schema errors, ready to paste into your CMS.
