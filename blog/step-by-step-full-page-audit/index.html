Step‑by‑Step Full‑Page SEO Audit with Detailed SEO & AI Engine

This guide walks you through installing the extension, running an audit, interpreting results, and exporting a shareable report.

1. Install & Verify

  1. Visit the Chrome Web Store link: Detailed SEO & AI Engine.
  2. Click “Add to Chrome”. After installation, a blue badge appears on the toolbar.

2. Run the Audit

  1. Open the page you want to analyze.
  2. Click the extension icon → select “Run Full Audit”.
  3. Wait ~5 seconds while the AI parses the DOM.

3. Interpret the Results

SectionWhat to Look ForQuick Fix
Meta TitleLonger than 60 chars or missing keywordEdit in CMS, keep ≤ 60 chars
Meta DescriptionLonger than 160 chars or duplicateRewrite, keep 150–160 chars
Heading TreeSkipped levels (H1 → H3)Add missing H2 tags
SchemaMissing “sameAs” or FAQ schemaInsert suggested JSON‑LD snippet
AI ScoreBelow 70 pointsEnhance content with target keywords

4. Export & Share

Click “Export PDF” to download a ready‑to‑share report. Attach it to client proposals or internal tickets.

5. Automate Repeated Audits

Enable “Batch Mode” in the extension settings, upload a CSV list of URLs, and let the extension process them one by one.
