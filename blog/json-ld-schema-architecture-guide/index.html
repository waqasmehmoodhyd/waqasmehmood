What is Semantic Schema Siloing and Why Does Google Prefer It?

Semantic schema siloing is the architectural practice of connecting related structured data entities—such as Organization, WebSite, WebPage, Author, and Article—inside a single nested @graph array, enabling search engine crawlers to parse entity relationships without resolving fragmented disjointed scripts.
