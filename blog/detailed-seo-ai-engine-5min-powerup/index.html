Detailed SEO & AI Engine: 5‑Minute SEO Power‑Up

The Detailed SEO & AI Engine Chrome extension brings a complete, AI‑driven SEO audit directly to your browser. In under five minutes you get insight that normally requires several separate tools.

Key Features & How to Use Them

  • One‑click full‑page audit – open any page, click the extension icon, and the AI scans headings, meta tags, schema, and content quality.
  • Interactive heading tree – visualises H1‑H6 hierarchy; click a heading to jump to that section for quick fixes.
  • JSON‑LD validator – highlights missing or malformed schema objects and suggests fixes.
  • Perplexity & relevance score – AI rates the page against 2026 SERP trends on a 0‑100 scale.
  • Export PDF/CSV – one‑click download of a ready‑to‑share report.

Benefits

Speed, accuracy, zero‑telemetry, and shareable reports make this extension ideal for marketers, agencies, and developers who need fast, reliable SEO data.

Potential Drawbacks & Mitigations

  • Limited to the currently loaded page – use the “Export All Tabs” option for batch audits.
  • Learning curve for JSON‑LD – built‑in tooltip provides quick schema snippets.
