Detailed SEO Extension: Common Warnings & Easy Fixes

How to interpret yellow warning lines, optimize title characters for Google SERP pixel limits, and resolve Chrome installation notices.

By Waqas Mehmood (Senior SEO Specialist) • 6 min read

Fix 1: Understanding the Chrome "Proceed with Caution" Notice

The Chrome "Proceed with caution: This extension is not trusted by Enhanced Safe Browsing" dialog is an automated informational notice displayed for newly published extensions. It does not indicate malicious code and is resolved by clicking "Continue to install".

When installing any newly published Chrome extension from the Web Store, users with Chrome's **Enhanced Safe Browsing** feature enabled may encounter an automated modal:

> *"Proceed with caution: This extension is not trusted by Enhanced Safe Browsing."*

This is standard Chrome Web Store policy for new extensions until they accumulate user history.

**How to Proceed Safely:** 1. Click **Continue to install** in the dialog box. 2. The extension installs immediately and is ready to use. 3. Verify that Detailed SEO & AI Engine has zero background network permissions and collects zero personal data.

Fix 2: Resolving Yellow-Line Title & Description Warnings

Yellow warning lines appear when meta title tags exceed 60 characters (or fall below 30 characters) and when meta descriptions exceed 160 characters. To achieve a green status badge, edit your content to meet standard SERP display limits.

The extension employs a color-coded diagnostic system: - **Green Badge:** Perfect character count within optimal search engine pixel boundaries. - **Yellow Warning:** Minor length violation that risks SERP truncation on mobile or desktop viewports. - **Red Alert:** Critical issue such as completely missing title, duplicate tags, or empty meta description.

**Recommended Character Thresholds:** - **Meta Title:** Keep between **50 and 60 characters** (or approximately 580 pixels). - **Meta Description:** Keep between **150 and 160 characters** (or approximately 960 pixels). - **H1 Tag:** Exactly one H1 tag per page matching your primary commercial topic.

  • ✓ Rewrite overly long titles by removing redundant brand slogans or filler words.
  • ✓ Front-load the primary keyword into the first 30 characters of the title.
  • ✓ Include an active verb and high-intent call-to-action in the meta description.

Fix 3: Resolving Schema Warnings & Subdomain Crawl Errors

Resolve schema warnings by injecting missing properties such as sameAs social entity links and author profiles. Ensure subdomain landing pages have dedicated sitemap.xml and robots.txt files to prevent Google Search Console crawl errors.

During technical audits, two common issues frequently trigger diagnostic warnings:

1. **Missing Entity Verification (sameAs):** When validating Person or Organization schemas, search engines look for external verification links. In the extension, missing `sameAs` links trigger an entity alert. Fix this by adding verified links to your LinkedIn, X, and Wikipedia entity profiles.

2. **Subdomain Sitemaps & 404 Handling:** If you host landing pages on subdomains (e.g., `ext.waqasmehmood.com`), ensure the subdomain serves: - A valid `robots.txt` allowing Googlebot indexation. - A dedicated `sitemap.xml` submitted to Google Search Console. - A custom `404.html` error document to prevent soft-404 crawl penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my title tag show yellow when it has 65 characters?

Google desktop search results truncate titles at approximately 600 pixels (roughly 60 characters). At 65 characters, your title will be cut off with an ellipsis in search results.

How do I submit my subdomain sitemap to Google Search Console?

Add your subdomain as a URL-prefix property in Google Search Console and submit the path /sitemap.xml under the Sitemaps tab.
