Frequently Asked Questions

Is it bad if one website links to me 500 times?
No, it is not harmful as long as the links are natural (e.g. a blogroll or credit link). However, search engines will treat those 500 links essentially as a single referring domain vote.
Why is referring domain count more important than total backlink count?
Google values votes from unique independent websites. Receiving 500 links from a single domain has diminishing returns, whereas receiving 1 link each from 500 unique trusted domains provides immense ranking authority.
What is a healthy referring domain growth velocity?
A natural, steady upward trajectory (e.g., 5 to 20 new high-quality referring domains per month) signals enduring brand authority without triggering unnatural link spike alarms.
