Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate SEO ROI?
SEO ROI is calculated as: ((Total Additional Organic Revenue - Total SEO Investment) / Total SEO Investment) × 100. For example, generating $60,000 in revenue from an $8,000 SEO investment yields a 650% ROI.
How long does it take for SEO to show a positive ROI?
Most commercial websites achieve breakeven and positive ROI within 3 to 6 months. By months 9 to 12, as high-difficulty commercial keywords reach top 3 positions, ROI typically scales exponentially.
What is PPC Equivalent Traffic Value?
PPC value represents what you would have had to pay Google Ads to acquire the same number of organic clicks. Generating 10,000 monthly organic clicks on keywords with an average CPC of $2 saves $20,000/month in paid ad spend.
Why does SEO have a higher long-term ROI than Paid Ads?
Paid ads require continuous spend for every visitor. When you stop paying, traffic drops to zero. With SEO, once your page ranks on Google, you receive hundreds or thousands of clicks daily at zero marginal cost.
