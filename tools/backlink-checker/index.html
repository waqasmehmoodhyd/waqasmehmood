Frequently Asked Questions

How many backlinks do I need to rank #1 on Google?
Quality far outweighs quantity. A single editorial backlink from a high-authority publication like Forbes, TechCrunch, or a university (.edu) domain often carries more weight than 500 low-quality directory links.
What makes a backlink high-quality in Google eyes?
A high-quality backlink comes from an authoritative, contextually relevant domain in your industry, passes real referral traffic, uses natural descriptive anchor text, and carries dofollow attribution.
Are nofollow links valuable for SEO?
Yes! Google treats nofollow, sponsored, and ugc link attributes as ranking hints. A natural, healthy backlink profile always contains a realistic mix of dofollow and nofollow citations.
