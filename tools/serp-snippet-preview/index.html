Frequently Asked Questions

How does Google calculate snippet truncation?
Google measures pixel width rather than raw character counts. Wide capital letters (like W, M) consume more pixel space than lowercase narrow characters (like i, l, t).
Why does Google sometimes rewrite my meta title or description?
If Google determines that your authored title is keyword-stuffed, too long, or fails to answer the user query as effectively as on-page H1 or H2 headings, its algorithms dynamically rewrite the snippet from visible page body copy.
How does rich snippet markup affect SERP preview appearance?
Adding valid JSON-LD schema (FAQPage, Product, Review, Breadcrumb) allows Google to render accordions, star ratings, and breadcrumb trails beneath your snippet, boosting CTR by 20% to 35%.
