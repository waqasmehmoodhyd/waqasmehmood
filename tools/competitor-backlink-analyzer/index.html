Frequently Asked Questions

What is a "Link Gap" in SEO strategy?
A link gap refers to websites that link to multiple competitors in your industry, but do not yet link to you. These represent your highest-probability outreach targets.
What is Link Gap Analysis?
Link gap analysis identifies authoritative industry websites that link to two or more of your direct organic competitors but not to your website. These represents high-probability link building opportunities.
How many backlinks do I need to outrank my competitor?
Raw quantity matters far less than relevance and domain authority. Earning 10 authoritative editorial links in your exact niche often outperforms 100 low-quality directory citations.
