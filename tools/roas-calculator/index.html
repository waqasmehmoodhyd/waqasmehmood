Frequently Asked Questions

What is ROAS and how is it calculated?
ROAS stands for Return on Ad Spend. It measures the gross revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising. Formula: Gross Revenue / Total Ad Spend. A 4.0x ROAS means you generate $4 in revenue for every $1 spent on ads.
What is the difference between ROAS and ROI?
ROAS calculates gross revenue divided by ad spend only. ROI (Return on Investment) considers all business expenses including cost of goods sold (COGS), shipping, and software fees to calculate true bottom-line net profit.
What is Breakeven ROAS?
Breakeven ROAS is the minimum return required so your campaign neither loses money nor makes a profit. It is calculated as 1 / Gross Profit Margin %. If your profit margin is 60%, your breakeven ROAS is 1 / 0.60 = 1.67x.
How can I improve my Google Ads ROAS?
You can improve ROAS through 3 core levers: 1) Negative keyword pruning to eliminate non-converting search terms, 2) Landing page CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) to turn more clicks into buyers, and 3) Value-based Smart Bidding with Target ROAS constraints.
