Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Free AI Math Solver completely free to use?
Yes, 100% free with no hidden paywalls, no daily problem limits, and no mandatory registration. You can solve unlimited algebra, arithmetic, quadratic, fraction, geometry, and calculus problems instantly.
How does this solver compare to Photomath and Solvely.ai?
While Photomath and Solvely lock detailed step-by-step derivations behind paid monthly subscriptions ($9.99–$19.99/mo), our tool provides complete step-by-step working, formula explanations, and interactive follow-up tutor assistance entirely free.
Can I upload photos of handwritten notes or screenshots?
Yes! Simply switch to the Photo tab and drag-and-drop an image, browse your device, or press Ctrl+V to paste a screenshot directly from your clipboard. The neural OCR will extract the equation and solve it.
What branches of mathematics are supported?
Our solver covers 10+ core disciplines: Arithmetic, Linear Algebra, Quadratic Equations, Fractions & Decimals, Percentages & Financial Ratios, Exponents & Powers, Radicals & Surds, Geometry (Area/Perimeter), Statistics (Averages), and Introductory Calculus (Derivatives & Integrals).
Does the solver show the full step-by-step working?
Yes! Every solution includes a massive, high-contrast final answer card, followed by numbered intermediate steps detailing each operation (such as adding or subtracting terms from both sides, finding common denominators, or applying the quadratic formula).
How does the tool verify that answers are correct?
The engine performs deterministic algebraic verification: it substitutes the calculated answer back into the original equation to verify that the left-hand side equals the right-hand side (e.g. for 3x + 6 = 18 with x = 4, verifying 3(4) + 6 = 18).
Can I ask follow-up questions if I don't understand a step?
Yes! Below every solution is an interactive AI Math Tutor. You can click 1-click prompts like "Explain in simpler words" or "Show an alternative method", or type your own question to receive tailored explanations.
Does this tool work on mobile devices?
Yes, the interface is 100% responsive and optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens with native touch support and camera photo uploads.
